Culminating project

Print-ready showcase poster

One large poster that shows the student’s growth as a digital creator. Submit a high-resolution file for classroom printing.

What to include

  • Personal logo used correctly, in the same corner as the rest of the course
  • 2–4 of the strongest designs, each with a short caption
  • Clear title and breathing room — do not fill every gap
  • High-resolution PDF Print or PNG
  • One or two sentences: What I’m most proud of…

Student steps — do these in order

  1. Create a large poster. In Canva click Create a design → Poster. Pick A3 or Custom size 11 × 17 in. Rename Showcase Poster – Your Name.
  2. Title the poster. Add a heading: My Design Journey, I Am a Digital Creator, or From Blank Page to Pro. Make it the largest text.
  3. Place your logo. Paste the Lesson 6 grouped logo into the same corner you used all term. Keep it small.
  4. Add 2–4 strongest pieces. Upload PNGs from earlier lessons or copy grouped designs. Caption each with one short line about the skill.
  5. Leave empty space. If the page feels busy, delete something. Hallway posters need air.
  6. Write the pride line. One or two sentences starting What I’m most proud of…
  7. Download for print. Share → Download → PDF Print (best) or a high-quality PNG. Hand that file in. Do not submit a tiny social-post size.

Suggested themes

  • My Design Journey
  • I Am a Digital Creator
  • From Blank Page to Pro

Printing checklist

  1. Collect PDF Print or high-quality PNG only — no screenshots.
  2. Open the file and zoom in. Photos should stay sharp.
  3. Confirm the logo and title stay readable at print size from a few steps back.
  4. Print on quality paper, colour if the school can.
  5. Display in the classroom or hallway. Invite students to stand by their poster for 30 seconds.
