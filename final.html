Culminating project

Print-ready showcase poster

One large poster that shows the student’s growth as a digital creator. Submit it for classroom printing.

What to include

  • Personal logo used correctly
  • 2–4 of the strongest designs
  • Clear title and breathing room
  • High-resolution PDF Print or PNG
  • One or two sentences: What I’m most proud of…

Suggested themes

  • My Design Journey
  • I Am a Digital Creator
  • From Blank Page to Pro

Printing checklist

  1. Export as PDF Print or high-quality PNG.
  2. Check that images stay sharp when zoomed.
  3. Confirm the logo and title stay readable at print size.
  4. Print on quality paper.
  5. Display in the classroom or hallway.
