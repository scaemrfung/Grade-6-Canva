Classroom

How the block works

Teach from this page. Students design in Canva Education. End every class with a saved PNG.

Before day one

  1. Complete Lesson 0 and verify Canva Education.
  2. Create a class and generate a join code.
  3. Put a blank name-card template in the class folder.
  4. Switch this site to Teacher view.

During the 30 minutes

  1. Warm-up. Students open yesterday’s design or Make a copy of a starter.
  2. Teach. Model one skill on the projector. Keep it short.
  3. Make. Students follow the numbered steps on the lesson page.
  4. Close. Download PNG, two volunteers share, name the next lesson.

Fast finishers

Every lesson has a Challenge. Use it for extra time, homework, or hallway display.
